## Known Gaps
Rows that are not 🟡, with the reason and the fix.

- **TR-data-003** ⚠️ Partial: The catalogue loads from an explicit manifest of data files, never a directory scan, and loads correctly from an Android export. Manifest part covered (D1: loader never lists a directory). Export part unverified: W2 is after acceptance and moved to an iOS export on iPhone; the Android leg is "deferred until an Android device is available" (ADR-0002 l.68). Nothing proves the Android clause.
- **TR-data-015** ⚠️ Partial: Load-time targets: Loading to Ready median at most 1000 ms on an Adreno 5xx-class device; edit-to-in-game at most 10 s. No decision allocates or guarantees the budget; only "small synchronous JSON, no import step" and a paper estimate (ADR-0002 l.457-459). W4 is after acceptance, advisory, and its Adreno leg is deferred in favour of iPhone (l.71). The 10 s loop has no verification item beyond GDD Acceptance 14.
- **TR-data-021** ❌ Gap: Record IDs are never changed or reused so saves can hold only IDs and levels, with a retired-ID list introduced at Vertical Slice for Save/Load migration. No ADR; deferred to Vertical Slice with Save/Load. Acceptable for MVP; write the ADR with the Save/Load design.
- **TR-input-005** ⚠️ Partial: Touch and mouse use one event family: emulation settings stop duplicate presses, the mouse pointer index never collides with touch index 0, non-left buttons are dropped. Family, emulation flags and non-left drop are covered. The no-collision clause is not guaranteed: D3 maps emulated mouse to `MOUSE_POINTER = 1000` only when SP5 can tell it apart by `device`, otherwise it keeps index 0 (stated deviation). On a touch-screen desktop a mouse press and touch 0 can share index 0
- **TR-state-003** ⚠️ Partial: Simulation time enters only through battle.tick(dt) at a fixed rate; no _process, Timers, await, wall clock or physics areas in gameplay; the wrapper clamps owed ticks. Rate, clamp (3, stall 0.5 s), and bans on `_physics_process`, `_process`, Timer, await, Time/OS are in D1, guidelines and lint. The "no physics areas" clause appears only in ADR-0004 Constraints (l.130); no Decision, guideline or lint rule bans `Area2D` or physics queries in gameplay code.
- **TR-state-013** ⚠️ Partial: Tests can enumerate a battle's systems, inject recording fakes, snapshot a battle, and fail when any battle signal has no listener (views on an allowlist). Enumerate (`Battle.emitters()`), snapshot (`digest`), listener-count test and self-proof are decided. Injecting recording fake *systems* into the battle's tick slots (GSF Acceptance 9 and 13, "recording systems A, B, C") has no decided seam: ADR-0007 D5 says "No new Battle API", ADR-0004 D4 hardcodes the six steps, and only ADR-0004 Validation 2 assumes the fakes exist. GSF Open Question l.277 is therefore only partly answered.
- **TR-state-018** ⚠️ Partial: A wave_cleared or waves_completed in the wrong phase, or an enemy_leaked in Build, Paused or after an outcome, is reported to the development error sink and ignored; an enemy_leaked in Intermission is reported and still applied. ADR-0001 D4 supplies the dev_error sink, but no ADR assigns this phase check to Flow. Fix: name it in ADR-0006 D5 (P1/W rows) or ADR-0004 D3.
- **TR-combat-013** ⚠️ Partial: Per-tick targeting stays within budget on a low-end phone at the catch-up clamp (about 400 range checks, under 2 ms, unmeasured). ADRs reject the GDD's 2 ms as unaffordable (one whole tick must fit 4.0 / N ms, 1.33 ms at N = 3) and register no targeting slice; the budget is a whole-tick figure, unmeasured until ADR-0009 V4 and ADR-0004 V6. The GDD still states 2 ms as an Open Question.
- **TR-hero-004** ⚠️ Partial: The builder injects halted(), in_build(), left_handed, the Hero record ID and burst_id into HeroSim; the wrapper passes halted, in_build, the hand and the stage to HeroInput, and the HUD reads burst_id() from HeroSim. ADR-0001 D5 gives the mechanism only and its B9 list (line 216) names no Hero ID or `burst_id`. The IDs reach HeroSim through ADR-0010 Key Interfaces (`HeroSim._init(..., hero_id, burst_id, left_handed, ...)`), HeroInput gets its values from the wrapper in ADR-0010 D8 (not the builder), and the HUD gets `burst_id` by querying `hero_sim.burst_id()` (ADR-0010 D7), not by injection. The registry text was reworded on 2026-11-05 to match; the ADR claim tables should now cite ADR-0010 D7, D8 and Key Interfaces.
- **TR-hud-010** ⚠️ Partial: The HUD reads Flow, Tower, Hero, Enemy & Wave and Combat via signals and new view-only queries; phase and lives_changed join the view allowlist. P1, P2, P3, G1 to G4, G8 and the B10 read surface are covered. Missing: (a) no ADR hands the stage's Wave records to the wrapper/HUD (ADR-0005 D8 gives `WaveRecord`s to EnemyWave only; `Battle` exposes `input_record`, `stage_record`, `visual_keys`), yet the next-wave line reads Wave records directly (HUD Rule 5, Interactions line 176); (b) HudLogic is not a listener of P7 `burst_started` although Rule 13 latches prompt 3 until `burst_started`
- **TR-hud-013** ⚠️ Partial: Debug builds append JSON lines to user://run_log.jsonl through an injectable sink; a write failure is non-fatal and release builds never write. Sink, file, debug-only `DevLogs`, never fatal: covered. But the attempt record must hold damage "by source and kind" (HUD Rule 19) and the log reads only the snapshot (D10), while `ResultSnapshot` (D11) carries only `StageResult` kind totals, no per-`source_id` damage (`Combat.dealt_by_id`). ADR-0006 D9 item 1 says "the HUD snapshot reads that", D11 does not
- **TR-hud-017** ❌ Gap: HudLogic reads the stage's Wave records directly, on phase change, to draw the next-wave line as one total per enemy type in fixed order. No ADR hands the Wave records to the wrapper or HUD (ADR-0005 D8 gives them to EnemyWave only). Fix: add a read-only Battle.wave_records or an EnemyWave view query (amend ADR-0005 D8 or ADR-0010 D7).
- **TR-hud-018** ⚠️ Partial: The HUD owns burst_button_centre(), computed from burst_y and handedness, as the single centre for the burst hit disc, Hero's cancel distances and the drawn ring. ADR-0010 D8 reads the centre once at attach, while ADR-0003 D5 can move the button when insets change mid-battle, so the drawn button and hit disc can part. Fix: freeze the bottom inset per battle, or re-push the centre to HeroInput on display_changed.

## Superseded Requirements

No requirement was split or retired in this review. Eleven entries were reworded to match GDD text changed after 2026-11-03 (same intent, `revised: 2026-11-05` in the registry): TR-data-005, TR-state-002, TR-state-007, TR-state-008, TR-input-004, TR-input-009, TR-input-013, TR-enemy-001, TR-enemy-004, TR-tower-012, TR-hero-004.
